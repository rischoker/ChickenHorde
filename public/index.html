FARM DEFENSE · FIELD GUIDE

How to Play

1 · Protect Mama Hen

Mama Hen sits on the Chicken Turret in the middle of the farm. Wolves, foxes, moles, eagles and worse come in waves to eat her. If her health reaches 0, the game is over. Survive as many waves as you can!

2 · Controls

Phone: scan the QR on the big screen. Left thumb moves, right thumb aims and fires automatically.

Keyboard + mouse: press PLAY ON THIS PC on the host screen, or open the join link on a laptop. WASD move · mouse aims · click or SPACE fires · arrow keys aim + fire.

Online: choose PLAY ONLINE on the start page, copy the invite and send it to up to 5 friends. Each one plays on their own computer and sees the game on their own screen.

3 · Grab power-ups

Defeated enemies drop crates: Shield, Medkit (heals you and Mama Hen), Double Shot, Rapid Fire, Chicken Turret, Chicken Laser, Chicken Seeker and the rare Chicken Hulk — a giant smashing chick for one wave. When Hulk ends you go back to normal.

4 · Teamwork & revives

If your chick goes down, a teammate must stand on you for 5 seconds to revive you. Stick together — more players means bigger waves!

5 · Bosses

Chupacabras (wave 5+) leaps over obstacles onto players after wave 7. Mushroom King (wave 8+) and Alien Overlord (wave 10+) have two health bars, resist every weapon and arrive with a shield that gets stronger with more players. The King rains spores that sprout kamikaze mushrooms; the Overlord blinks, summons 3 illusions and raises a prismatic shield.

Fire Lord (wave 11+) is almost invisible — look for the faint embers! It ignores you, walks to Mama Hen and unleashes a deadly sonic scream cone. Low health: shooting it reveals it. Crazy Rabbit (wave 13+) zooms in at super speed, drops carrot bombs near Mama Hen and runs away. Gnome mages chase Mama Hen until you hit them.

6 · Night falls at wave 10

Waves 10–19 happen at night, 20–29 by day, 30–39 at night again… At night fire tornados roam the fields (just for show). From wave 10 the Mecha Frog joins the horde: it arrives shielded, leaps across the field, fires fast homing rockets and explodes when destroyed. Ghosts can turn ethereal and ignore damage for a moment. Stay sharp!
